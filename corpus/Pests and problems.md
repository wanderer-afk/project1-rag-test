# Pests and problems

## Common pests
Spider mites leave fine webbing and tiny speckles on leaves. Wipe the leaves with a damp cloth and rinse the plant. Mealybugs look like small white cotton tufts in leaf joints. Dab them with rubbing alcohol on a cotton bud.

## Yellow leaves
A few yellow lower leaves are normal as a plant ages. Many yellow leaves together usually mean too much water or too little light. Check the soil before you water again.
