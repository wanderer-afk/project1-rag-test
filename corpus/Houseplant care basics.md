# Houseplant care basics

## Watering
Most houseplants prefer the soil to dry out a little between waterings. Push a finger two centimetres into the soil. If it feels dry, water thoroughly until water runs out of the drainage holes, then empty the saucer. Overwatering is the most common way plants die, because the roots need air as well as water.

## Light
Bright indirect light suits most leafy plants. A window facing east or west usually works well. Direct afternoon sun can scorch leaves on many species. If new growth is small and pale, the plant probably needs more light.
