# Repotting and soil

## When to repot
Repot when roots grow out of the drainage holes or the plant dries out within a day of watering. Move up only one pot size, about 2 to 3 centimetres wider, so the soil stays dry enough for the roots.

## Choosing soil
Use a well-draining potting mix. Add perlite or bark for chunky mixes such as those for orchids and monstera. A pot without drainage holes holds too much water, even if the soil looks right.
